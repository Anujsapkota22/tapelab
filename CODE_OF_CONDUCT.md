# Code of Conduct

TapeLab is committed to a welcoming and constructive community. Participants should be respectful, assume good faith, discuss technical disagreements directly, and focus feedback on ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Project maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate these standards and may restrict participation when needed to keep the community safe.

To report a concern, contact the project maintainers privately through GitHub. Please include enough context to understand what happened. Reports will be handled with care and as confidentially as the circumstances allow.
